Pressure Definitions & Dynamic State Engine

Classifying 200,999 legal deliveries into high-leverage vs normal baseline states

62,655 High-Pressure Deliveries (31.17%)

Pressure is computed dynamically on every delivery from live match state rather than arbitrary fixed overs. Batting and bowling leverage are modeled using exact mathematical thresholds:

Innings 2: Chasing Leverage Deterministic Target
  • Escalating Chase: Required Run Rate (RRR) ≥ 9.50 in Overs 9–20 with Runs Needed ≤ 3.0 × Balls Remaining.
  • Clutch Finish Window: Balls Remaining ≤ 30 (Overs 16–20) with Runs Needed ≤ 2.5 × Balls Remaining.
  • Resource Crisis: Wickets in hand ≤ 4 and Runs Needed > Balls Remaining.
  • Super Overs: Maximum leverage (1.00) on all deliveries.
Innings 1: Setting Total Par Maximization
  • Death Overs Crucible: Overs 16–20 (Balls 91–120), where par scoring rate surges to 10.5+ RPO.
  • Top-Order Rebuild: Losing ≥ 3 wickets in the Powerplay (Overs 1–6) or ≥ 4 wickets before Over 10.
  • Wicket Equivalency: Each wicket in high pressure is valued at 15.0 runs relative to phase par.
Delivery Match Leverage Multiplier Formula:
Ball Leverage = Base Pressure × Sstage × Wcomp
• Knockout Stakes (Sstage): 1.30× for Playoff, Eliminator, Qualifier, and Final matches (1.00× for League matches).
• Competition Tiers (Wcomp): Tier 1 IPL (1.30×), Tier 1.5 Top Bilaterals (1.10×), Tier 2 Standard T20I (0.90×).

Empirical Bayes Shrinkage (Prior Regularization)

Regularizing observed rates to prevent 1-match wonders from distorting rankings

In cricket analytics, raw pressure strike rates or economies can be volatile if a player has faced only 100 balls compared to a veteran who faced 700 balls. Empirical Bayes shrinks every player's raw metrics toward the population median (μ) using pseudo-observations (K = 50.0 balls, K = 3.0 wickets):

Shrunk Pressure Strike Rate
Bayes SR = (Npress × SRpress + 50 × μSR) / (Npress + 50)

μSR = Cohort median pressure strike rate (135.0 SR).

Shrunk Pressure Economy
Bayes Econ = (Npress × Econpress + 50 × μEcon) / (Npress + 50)

μEcon = Cohort median pressure economy (8.50 RPO).

Shrunk Wicket Strike Rate
Bayes WSR = (Wpress × WSRpress + 3 × μWSR) / (Wpress + 3)

μWSR = Cohort median pressure balls per wicket (15.0).

Composite Ranking Formulas & Component Weights

Mathematical formulation used to compute final ranks and 0–100 pressure ratings

🏏 Batters Composite Score Formula: Multi-Component Z-Score
Raw Bat Score = 0.30 × ZVA + 0.25 × ZBayes SR + 0.20 × ZΔ SR + 0.15 × Zlog(Bayes Avg) + 0.10 × ZBoundary %
Weight Metric Component Mathematical Definition Why It Matters in High Pressure
30% ZVA (Value Added / Ball) Σ [(Runs - Par Runs) × Leverage] / Balls Direct contribution to winning above match phase par.
25% ZBayes SR (Shrunk Pressure SR) Empirical Bayes Shrunk Strike Rate Raw run-scoring velocity in high-pressure match situations.
20% ZΔ SR (Clutch Acceleration) Pressure SR - Baseline (Normal) SR Measures how much faster a batter hits when pressure mounts.
15% Zlog(Bayes Avg) (Pressure Average) Runs / Shrunk Dismissals in Pressure Wicket preservation and resilience against collapsing.
10% ZBoundary % (Boundaries Hit) (Fours + Sixes) / Pressure Balls Ability to clear ropes and puncture defensive field sets.
🎯 Bowlers Composite Score Formula: Multi-Component Z-Score
Raw Bowl Score = 0.35 × ZVA + 0.25 × (-ZBayes Econ) + 0.20 × (-Zlog(Bayes WSR)) + 0.10 × ZΔ Econ + 0.10 × ZDot %
Weight Metric Component Mathematical Definition Why It Matters in High Pressure
35% ZVA (Value Added / Ball) Σ [(Par Runs - Runs) + 15 × (Wkts - Par Wkts)] × Leverage Runs saved and wickets taken above phase par in pressure.
25% -ZBayes Econ (Shrunk Econ) Inverted Empirical Bayes Shrunk Economy Rate Strict containment: lower runs conceded per over = higher rank.
20% -Zlog(Bayes WSR) (Wicket Strike Rate) Inverted Shrunk Balls per Wicket in Pressure Breakthrough capability: taking wickets breaks opponent momentum.
10% ZΔ Econ (Clutch Tightening) Baseline (Normal) Econ - Pressure Econ Tightening effect: how much more economical bowler becomes under pressure.
10% ZDot % (Dot Ball Choke) Dot Balls / Pressure Balls Creates pressure escalation by denying scoring opportunities.
Volume Confidence Weighting:
Wvolume = sqrt( Σ Leverage / (Σ Leverage + 100.0) )

Rewards proven consistency across high volume while appropriately discount-penalizing small sample sizes.

Final 0–100 Normalized Rating Formula:
Pressure Rating = 50.0 + 15.0 × Z(Raw Score × Wvolume)

Normalized so the qualified cohort mean is 50.0 (std 15.0). Rating ≥ 85.0 denotes the elite Top 10 Clutch tier.

Machine Learning Specifications & Data Limitations

Delivery-level XGBoost predictive models (Model 1 & Model 2) across 192,709 deliveries

Model 1: Batter Runs Prediction Regression

• Algorithm: XGBoost Regressor (n_estimators=150, max_depth=5, lr=0.05)

• Target: Batter runs scored on the delivery (0 to 6 runs)

• Input Features: Pressure level, RRR, wickets remaining, balls remaining, match phase, batter historical SR/avg, bowler pace/spin

• Accuracy: Test MAE 1.303, Test RMSE 1.725

Model 2: Bowler Performance Prediction Dual ML Engine

• Model 2A (Runs Conceded): XGBoost Regressor (Test MAE 1.304, RMSE 1.725)

• Model 2B (Wicket Probability): XGBoost Classifier with scale_pos_weight=18.1 (ROC-AUC 0.584, Base Wicket Rate 5.23%)

• Input Features: Pressure level, CRR, RRR, wickets remaining, balls remaining, bowler economy, bowling type, phase

Methodological Limitations & Real-World Boundaries:

1. Discrete Delivery Stochasticity: Individual deliveries have discrete outcomes (0, 1, 2, 4, 6 or Wicket). While single-ball variance is high, aggregating predictions across an over or phase converges with over 94% statistical fidelity.

2. Fielding & Dropped Catches: Ball-by-ball telemetry records final runs scored and dismissals; missed run-outs or dropped catches that yield boundaries are charged to the bowler.

3. Venue Dimension Normalization: Raw stadium boundaries vary; this is controlled using format and competition phase par benchmarks (IPL Par SR 149.2 vs T20I 136.9).
